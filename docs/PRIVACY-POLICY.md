# Privacy policy — frontier go

_Last updated: 21 September 2026_

frontier go collects nothing about you.

That is the whole policy. The rest of this page explains what that means in
practice, because "we value your privacy" is what every app says while doing the
opposite.

## There is no account

There is no sign-in, no email address, no profile, no username. The app has no
server of its own, so there is nowhere for information about you to go.

## The app never asks where you are

frontier go shows a globe, and it still does not request location permission.
The geography you see belongs to the **footage** — where a dive happened, where
a rocket was tested — not to you. The app does not know what country you are in
and does not try to find out.

## What stays on your device

- which places you have been, and when
- what you have saved
- your channel, mute and ambient-mode preferences
- a capped list of technical errors, shown read-only in Profile

All of it is stored by iOS on your phone. None of it is transmitted anywhere.
Deleting the app deletes all of it.

## What leaves your device

Only requests for the video you are watching, made directly to the publishing
agency's own servers:

- `oceanexplorer.noaa.gov` (NOAA Ocean Exploration)
- `images-assets.nasa.gov` (NASA Image and Video Library)

Those agencies see what any web server sees when a file is requested: an IP
address, a time, and which file. frontier go sends them nothing else — no
identifier, no account, no history. Their own privacy practices are theirs, and
they are U.S. federal agencies subject to federal privacy rules.

The catalog of what is available ships inside the app. It is not fetched, so the
app does not report what you browse to anyone, including us.

## No analytics, no tracking, no advertising

There is no analytics SDK, no crash reporter, no advertising identifier, no
attribution framework, no third-party tracker of any kind. The performance
figures on the Profile screen are computed on your phone, live in memory, and
disappear when the app closes.

## Children

frontier go is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone,
regardless of age.

## Changes

If this ever changes, the change will appear here and in the App Store privacy
labels before a version that behaves differently is released.
