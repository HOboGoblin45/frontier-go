# Buyers and partners

Who would pay for frontier go, what each one would be buying, and how to
start. Approach only once the milestones in the plan §5 are met; before that,
the right conversation is a partnership, not a sale.

Facts marked **[V]** were checked against a primary source; **[R]** are from
research passes and not re-checked.

## Strategic, in order of fit

| Who | Why frontier go fits | Evidence they buy | First move |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Blue Ant Media / MagellanTV** | Factual nature, science and space streaming; an ambient, rights-clean, free-to-user front door to their audience | Bought MagellanTV for $12M ($6M upfront, $6M over two years) **[V]** | Partnership on the TV app or a co-branded channel, not a cold pitch |
| **CuriosityStream** | Same audience, same genre; a lean-back product they do not have | Operates FAST channels and licenses factual libraries **[R]** | Business-development contact about a free ambient channel |
| **Simulation Curriculum** (SkySafari, Starry Night) | Space-education software; a globe and a curated space catalog extend their range | Acquired SkySafari **[R]** | Product partnership |
| **Ambient-TV and FAST operators** | The app is already built for AirPlay and Ambient mode; the catalog is a ready channel | FAST channel market growth **[R]** | License the catalog and pipeline as a channel |
| **Museums, aquariums, planetariums** | Lobby and exhibit screens; privacy-clean, free, no ads | Institutional buyers of display content **[R]** | Licensing the pipeline, not the app |

## Marketplace fallback

| Where | Fit |
| --- | --- |
