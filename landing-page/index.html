frontier go
Real places. Bigger perspective.

Go somewhere extraordinary.

A continuously playing window into extraordinary places on Earth and beyond. Open it and you are already somewhere. Tap Shuffle and you are somewhere else.

Free. No ads, no account, no tracking.

What it does

No decisions

No catalog, no search, no categories, no account. The first thing you see is footage, and it keeps playing by itself.

Shuffle is transportation

A deep-sea octopus, then a rocket test, then Antarctic ice, then a spacewalk. Every jump is somewhere genuinely different.

The globe

An interactive Earth showing where there is something to watch, where you have been, and where you are now.

Keep exploring here

One tap narrows everything to this expedition, mission or region. One tap widens it again.

Collections

Whole deep-sea expeditions, Mars rovers, Apollo, spacewalks, engines on the test stand. New footage arrives every week.

Fall asleep somewhere else

A sleep timer and an ambient mode that hides everything but the picture. Three kilometres down, very quietly.

Made for a television

AirPlay, Picture in Picture, lock screen controls and background audio, because playback is native rather than a web page pretending.

A quiet record

The Discovery Passport remembers where you have been. No points, no streaks, no badges.

Where the footage comes from

Every clip is public-domain or openly licensed material published by the organisation credited on it. Each one shows its source, its credit line and the terms it is offered under. Anything whose rights could not be confirmed is left out.

Usage terms

Private by construction

No account. No tracking. No advertising. frontier go never asks for your location — the geography belongs to the footage, not to you. What you have watched and saved stays on your phone, and there is no server for it to go to.
