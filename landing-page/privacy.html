Privacy

Last updated 22 September 2026

frontier go collects nothing about you.

That is the whole policy. The rest of this page explains what it means in practice, because "we value your privacy" is what every app says while doing the opposite.

There is no account

No sign-in, no email address, no profile, no username. The app has no server of its own, so there is nowhere for information about you to go.

The app never asks where you are

frontier go shows a globe, and it still does not request location permission. The geography you see belongs to the footage — where a dive happened, where a rocket was tested — not to you. The app does not know what country you are in and does not try to find out.

What stays on your device

  • which places you have been, and when
  • what you have saved
  • your channel, mute, ambient-mode and sleep-timer preferences
  • the most recent copy of the footage catalog
  • a capped list of technical errors, shown read-only in the app

All of it is stored by iOS on your phone. None of it is transmitted anywhere. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

What leaves your device

Only requests for the video you are watching, made directly to the publishing agency's own servers: oceanexplorer.noaa.gov and images-assets.nasa.gov. Those agencies see what any web server sees when a file is requested: an IP address, a time, and which file. frontier go sends them nothing else.

A catalog of what is available ships inside the app. Once a launch, the app also asks this website for a newer copy of that catalog, so new footage can arrive without an app update. That request is the same for every user, carries nothing about you, and is not logged by us. The website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which, like any host, sees an IP address and a time.

The app does not report what you watch, save or browse to anyone, including us.

Shared links

If you share a discovery, the link points to a page on this website that plays the clip in a browser. Opening it is an ordinary web visit. The page sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

No analytics, no tracking, no advertising

There is no analytics SDK, no crash reporter, no advertising identifier, no attribution framework and no third-party tracker of any kind. The performance figures shown on the Profile screen are computed on your phone, live in memory, and disappear when the app closes.

Children

frontier go is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, regardless of age.

Changes

If this ever changes, the change will appear here and in the App Store privacy labels before a version that behaves differently is released.
