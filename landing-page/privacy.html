Your privacy

Privacy policy

Effective September 21, 2026. Last updated September 21, 2026.

What stays on your device

Trailer Roulette has no accounts, analytics SDKs or advertising identifiers. Your saved movies, filters, sound setting, policy acceptance and recent diagnostic errors are stored locally using device preferences. We do not upload this local list or a viewing profile.

Network requests and third parties

The app uses YouTube API Services. Trailers play in YouTube's official privacy-enhanced embedded player in an Apple WKWebView. YouTube and Google receive playback requests and standard network information, such as your IP address and device/browser information. They may use cookies or similar storage and show their own ads. See the Google Privacy Policy and YouTube Terms of Service. We do not block or replace YouTube ads.

TMDB receives movie, trailer, filter and image requests, including standard network information. Where-to-watch information is supplied by JustWatch through TMDB. Vercel hosts this site and the player page and may retain standard request logs. Trailer video streams directly from YouTube; we do not host or download it. See TMDB privacy and Vercel privacy.

Location and Theater Mode

Theater Mode is not enabled in the public 3.5.0 build, and that build does not request location. In development builds with Theater Mode enabled, Near me requests a one-time location to sort theaters on your device. Coordinates are not stored or sent to us or theater feeds. Those builds request market schedules from Alamo Drafthouse using a market identifier. Permission is optional.

Sharing, external links and support

Sharing sends the movie information you choose to the destination you select in the system share sheet. External movie, ticket and service links are governed by their destinations' privacy policies. No affiliate commission is enabled in 3.5.0. If you email support, we receive your email address and the content you send. We use it to respond and resolve your request; do not include passwords or other sensitive information.

Diagnostics and deletion

Recent app errors remain on your device unless you choose to include them in a support email. Apple may provide developer diagnostics according to your device settings and its privacy policy. Removing the app deletes its app-local preferences; device backups and third-party records are managed separately. Contact us about deleting support correspondence. We retain it only as needed to resolve requests and meet applicable obligations.

Audience and changes

This app is not directed at children under 13. Movie trailers can contain violence, strong language and mature themes. Check the App Store age rating and use parental controls. We publish policy changes here and request agreement again for material revisions in the app.
