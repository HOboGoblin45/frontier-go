Support

Something not working, or a place you think belongs in the app? Write to crescicharles@gmail.com.

Every message is read by the person who builds the app.

The picture is black but I can hear sound

Open Profile and scroll to Diagnostics. If Video surface does not say transparent, please send a screenshot of that section.

A clip will not play

Some clips are served by the agency that published them and occasionally go missing from their servers. Tap Shuffle; the app remembers the failure and stops offering that clip. New footage is added every week.

Watching on a television

Tap the AirPlay button at the top of the player, choose your TV, then turn on Ambient mode from the down arrow to hide everything but the picture. The moon button sets a sleep timer.

Does it cost anything?

No. frontier go is free, has no advertising and no in-app purchases, and needs no account.

Where does the footage come from?

From NOAA Ocean Exploration and NASA, which publish it as public-domain material. Every clip carries its source and credit line inside the app. frontier go is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by either agency. If you believe a clip should not be in the app, write to the address above and it will be reviewed promptly.

Your data

There is none to delete: frontier go has no account and keeps your history on your device only. See the privacy policy.
