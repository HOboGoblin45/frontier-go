A little help

Support

Email crescicharles@gmail.com. Include the app version from About, your device and iOS version, and the steps that went wrong.

A trailer stops or will not play

Check your connection, use Skip in the player, or close the player with Done and press Play again. Some videos restrict playback by region or device. If the channel consistently stops between trailers, tell us which movie and whether an ad played first.

No movies load

Reconnect to the internet and tap Try again. The app also retries automatically. Clear filters if the selected mix is too narrow.

AirPlay

Use a compatible receiver on the same network. Start a trailer and select the receiver with AirPlay. Availability can vary with the receiver and the video.

Where is Theater Mode?

It is not included in the public 3.5.0 build while we arrange permission for theater programming data.

Saved movies and privacy

Save a movie from About this movie. Saved selections stay on this device. There are no accounts or cloud sync. Read our Privacy policy.

Troubleshooting details

Open About, then Troubleshooting details to see plugin status and recent local errors. Share only details relevant to your issue. YouTube may show its own ads; Trailer Roulette cannot remove them.
