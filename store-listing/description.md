# App Store listing — frontier go

Everything below is what is in App Store Connect. Written 2026-09-22 for the
first public release (4.2.0); description updated 2026-09-23 for 4.4.0 (national
parks, wildlife and history). The reasoning is in
`docs/GROWTH-AND-ACQUISITION-PLAN.md` §6.

Rules this copy follows:

- **No "NASA" or "NOAA" in the name, subtitle or keywords.** NASA's advertising
  guidelines prohibit its name alongside a product name, Apple forbids
  company names in keywords (2.3.7), and "nasa" is owned by NASA's own app
  anyway. The agencies are credited, factually, in the description.
- **No implied endorsement**, and a plain statement that there is none.
- **Terms and privacy links at the end of the description**, because the app
  uses Apple's standard licence agreement, which App Store product pages do not
  link on their own.

## Description

```
frontier go is a continuously playing window into extraordinary places on Earth and beyond: wildlife, wild places, landmarks, history, the deep sea and space.

Open it and you are already somewhere: three kilometres down a Pacific seamount, elk bugling in the Rockies, a geyser basin at dawn, San Francisco the morning after the 1906 earthquake, a hurricane seen from orbit. Tap Shuffle and you are somewhere else.

Free. No ads. No account. Nothing about you leaves your phone.

NO DECISIONS REQUIRED
There is no catalog to browse and no account to make. The first thing you see is footage, and it keeps playing by itself, like a channel, for as long as you leave it on.

SHUFFLE IS TRANSPORTATION
Every jump is somewhere genuinely different: a deep-sea octopus, then a Civil War battlefield, then desert tortoises, then Mars. The app deliberately puts distance between one place and the next.

COLLECTIONS
When you want more of something, stay a while. Birds, mammals, reptiles, fish, insects, plants and fungi. Canyons, geysers, caves, lighthouses. The Civil War, the Revolution, some of the first films ever made. Hundreds of national parks and historic sites, whole deep-sea expeditions, Apollo. New footage arrives every week.

THE GLOBE
An interactive Earth showing where there is something to watch and where you have been. Filter it to animals, plants, landscapes, landmarks or history, tap any place, and see everything filmed there and nearby. Every pin comes from the source's own records and says how precise it is.

MADE FOR THE TELEVISION
AirPlay to the big screen, Picture in Picture, lock-screen controls and background audio. Turn on Ambient mode to hide everything but the picture, and set the sleep timer to fall asleep three kilometres down.

YOUR DISCOVERY PASSPORT
A quiet record of the places you have been. No points, no streaks, no badges.

WHERE THE FOOTAGE COMES FROM
Every clip is public-domain footage published by the National Park Service, the Library of Congress, NOAA Ocean Exploration or NASA, shown with its credit line and a link to its source. Anything whose rights could not be confirmed is left out. frontier go is an independent app and is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by any of them or by any government agency.

Terms of Use: https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/
Privacy Policy: https://hobogoblin45.github.io/frontier-go/privacy
```

## Age rating

4+. Documentary footage of places, animals, machines and historical events; anything graphic or disturbing is excluded at ingest. No violence, no
mature themes, no user-generated content, no unrestricted web access (links
open in Safari), no gambling, no purchases.

## App Privacy

**Data Not Collected.** The app has no account, no analytics SDK, no
advertising identifier and no server of its own. Its only network requests are
for the video being played (agency and Library of Congress servers) and for the public catalog file
(GitHub Pages), neither of which carries anything about the user.
