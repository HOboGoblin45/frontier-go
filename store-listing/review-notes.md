# App Review notes — frontier go 4.2.0

Paste into App Store Connect → App Review Information → Notes.

```
frontier go is free: no account, no sign-in, no ads, no in-app purchases. There is nothing to log in to; the app opens straight into playing footage.

WHAT IT IS
A continuously playing channel of real exploration footage: deep-ocean ROV dives and space-mission footage. Shuffle jumps to a deliberately different place; Explore has an interactive globe of where footage was shot and 36 collections (whole expeditions and subjects); a sleep timer and Ambient mode are made for AirPlay to a television.

CONTENT AND RIGHTS (5.2.1 / 5.2.2)
Every clip is a U.S. Government work published by NOAA Ocean Exploration (oceanexplorer.noaa.gov) or NASA (images.nasa.gov), streamed directly from the agency's own servers. Works of the U.S. Government are not subject to copyright (17 U.S.C. 105). NOAA: "All video on the portal is in the public domain and should be credited to NOAA Ocean Exploration" (oceanexplorer.noaa.gov/about/media-kit). NASA: "NASA content ... generally are not subject to copyright in the United States" (nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media).
The catalog is built by an automated pipeline that fails closed: any item with a copyright marker, a third-party credit, a music or licence marker, or unknown rights is excluded, and the same rights check runs again inside the app. Every clip shows its credit line and source link. No agency logo or insignia is used as app branding, and the app states that it is not affiliated with or endorsed by NASA or NOAA (Profile screen and App Store description).

HOW TO TEST
1. Launch: footage starts after one tap on "Start Exploring".
2. Tap Shuffle a few times.
3. Tap the title at the top of the player to change channel.
4. Explore tab -> Collections -> pick "Octopus and squid".
5. Moon button -> set a sleep timer.
6. AirPlay button (top right) to send video to a TV.
7. Profile -> About for support, privacy and terms links.

Network: the app needs an internet connection to stream video. It works on cellular and Wi-Fi.
```
