# TestFlight — What to Test, 4.2.0

```
frontier go 4.2.0 - the version going to the App Store.

New
- Explore -> Collections: whole deep-sea expeditions and subjects (octopus and squid, Mars, vents, spacewalks, storms from above...). Pick one; it plays shuffled until you tap Go Anywhere.
- Tap the title at the top of the player to change channel.
- Moon button: sleep timer (15/30/60/90 min or end of clip).
- Share (i -> Share): the link opens a web page that plays the clip, with a button to get the app. Send one to yourself and open it on another device.
- Profile -> About: share the app, support, privacy, terms.

Please check
1. Collections open and play the right kind of footage.
2. Sleep timer pauses playback when it runs out.
3. A shared link opens the clip in Safari, and "Open in the app" jumps back into the app on the same clip.
4. Titles look like titles, not file names.
5. Leave it running and confirm new clips keep coming.
```
