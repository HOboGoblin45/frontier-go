# What's New — v4.0.0

```
frontier go.

This app began as Trailer Roulette. It has become something else: a
continuously playing window into real, extraordinary places. Deep-ocean dives
from NOAA Ocean Exploration. Earth from orbit, rocket engine tests, spacewalks
and Mars from NASA.

Open it and you are already somewhere. Tap Shuffle and you are somewhere else.

Playback is now fully native, so AirPlay to a television, Picture in Picture and
lock screen controls all work properly, there are no advertisements between
clips, and Shuffle is instant because the next place is already loaded.

New:
- The Frontier Globe. Turn the Earth, pick a place, go there.
- Keep Exploring Here. Stay with one expedition, mission or region.
- The Discovery Passport. A quiet record of where you have been.
- Ambient mode. Everything but the picture disappears and it keeps playing.

Every clip shows where it came from, who recorded it and the terms it is offered
under. No account. No tracking. No location permission.
```
